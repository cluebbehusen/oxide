# 0.13 — the weld campaign (2026-07-29)

> Part of the [experiment log](README.md): the working lab notebook
> of Oxide's bot-training and balance campaigns, kept as it was
> written. Newest entries at the bottom.

The campaign that trained once under everything 0.13 changed — the
0.12 movement overhaul, terrain-only cover, the wreck economy,
builder crews and deferred founding, unit repair, the Repair Bay,
the roster at par — and ended with a verdict no campaign here has
returned before: **the candidate does not ship.** The frozen
artifact (the 0.11 lineage widened to the v6 contract, embedded
digest `7e6f1303875b983e`) remains the shipped opponent, and the
[sim-batch entry](2026-07-28-the-0.13-sim-batch.md)'s "pre-campaign
baseline" is therefore not a baseline anymore — it is the shipped
game's standing description, and stays so until a future campaign
clears the battery this one failed.

## The terms

Contract v6: 65 features, 24 actions — RepairUnit and the Repair
Bay's build slot appended (the reclaim-parity rule), with
`damaged_unit_value` joining the features so a potential can price
what welding recovers. The shipped artifact took the widen bridge
(zero columns, unreachable floors; every fixture held unblessed);
the float side got reachable zero biases, per the 0.11 ritual. The
bars the campaign existed to take back: Expert's outright yardstick
sweep and the fog-honest duel per-seat floor, on loan since the
movement era.

## No parent survived: the from-scratch lineage

Every prior campaign resumed an intact parent. This one could not —
no parent survived into the 0.13 world — so the lineage is from
scratch, with the 0.11 fresh-league dials verbatim:

    bc.py --arch deep --episodes 48 --out runs/prior013.pt
    league.py --name c013a --resume prior013 --anchor prior013
      --anchor-coef 0.05 --anchor-decay 1.0 --maps grand

## The canary record (evals every 25 updates, probes every 100)

- **Rusher** held 1.0 through most of the 2000 updates, with
  scattered 0.5 dips and outright 0.0 at updates 100, 1150, 1600
  and 1725 — soft spots, never the sustained collapse that marks
  shelf-life drift.
- **Prime sat pinned in {0.0, 0.5} for the entire run.** 0.5 is one
  deterministic greedy game per orientation, won from one chair and
  lost from the other — the seat-sided signature was visible from
  update 25 and no later checkpoint cured it.
- **Composition p10 ranged 1.0-1.33** across every in-loop probe
  (50 matches at 20k, quantized snapshot): never within reach of
  the shipped artifact's 1.55 floor. Entropy 1.79-2.17, decided
  21-37 of 50 — the passivity that later killed the gate battery,
  legible in the run log from the first probe. The in-loop
  composition probe shipped this cycle and earned its keep on its
  first campaign: the canaries forecast the verdict 1900 updates
  before the tournament confirmed it.
- **The seeded verbs took.** ep_teched ~96% of episodes throughout.
  ep_salvage grew from 9% of episodes in the first 250 updates to
  40-47% AFTER the bonus annealed to zero — under wreck-economy
  prices the true objective sustains salvage in stochastic play
  unaided. ep_repair held steady at 6-8% with its bonus long gone:
  kept, at the rate welding actually pays.

is that the v6 verbs are learnable and economically self-sustaining
in training; the next campaign inherits that evidence, not the
weights.

## The gate battery: three gates down

All runs on the release driver, candidate embedded or `--weights` as
each instrument takes it.

1. **neural-cup, 150 seeds x 2 seats x 4 tiers (1,200 games):
   1050/1200, ZERO draws.** The zero-draw rule holds; the miss is
   Prime at 150/300 — seat-sided, the artifact as Ferrous wins its
   one deterministic greedy game and as Cupric loses it, replicated
   across all 150 seeds. The 0.9/0.11 precedent (1200/1200) is not
   matched.
2. **fun gate: OPEN** — entropy 2.02 >= 1.8, tech kinds 32.2% >=
   25%, fattest tech kind lancer 14.0% >= 3%. But the probe's
   decided cohort is 94/150 (37.3% censored) against the frozen
   artifact's 126/150 — the stall shadow already showing.
3. **The ladder disorders at shipped dials**: widened yardstick Easy
   100, Medium 176, **Hard 134**, Expert 210. Recalibration probes
   found the patience artifact again (more skill at c20 measures
   WORSE: 850→134, 900→129, 950→124) and the wound in cadence
   (Hard at trained c16: 850→192, 900→200 — both cleanly between
   Medium and Expert). Re-meterable — but the decisiveness sweep
   collapsed: **skirmish Medium 31/48 decided, 17 stalls** against
   the 48/48 bar, and re-dialing Medium to the trained cadence read
   worse (27/48). The stalls are the policy, not the dial.
4. **The loaned bars are not carried.** Expert's cadence grid at
   skill 1000 — c8 198, c10 150, c12 210, c14 180, c16 210, c18
   210, c20 120 — loses the Prime off-seat game at EVERY dial;
   70/80 is the artifact's ceiling and the outright sweep bar is
   unreachable. The fog-honest duel (scripted vs classic,
   weights-independent) reads [13, 9] per seat, combined 22/40 —
   the whipsaw's fourth reading ([6,15] → [11,15] → [17,4] →
   [13,9]); a per-seat floor restores only at 9, below majority.
5. **sweep-factorial mixed-roster marginal: Ferrous 30.1%
   [27.4, 33.0]** against the sim-batch reference 48.5%
   [46.0, 51.1], with 730/3072 undecided (23.8% censored vs 2.3%).
   The sim is untouched — the skew is the artifact's
   faction-conditional play. The Repair Bay resurrects nothing:
   garrison spot-checks crack at the shipped prices (the R4 verdict
   stands), and the stalls are mirror passivity, not bay healing.
6. **The bless was correctly not reached** — pinning fixtures to a
   non-shipping artifact would pin the wrong weights. The tree went
   back to HEAD byte-identical.

Verdict: **blocked.** The first campaign of the neural era to end
with no new artifact.

## The reading

**Strength was never the failure — decisiveness was.** A
from-scratch league under the new physics farmed checkpoints that
sweep four opponents 60/60, and the short consolidation closed the
last scripted hole exactly on the 0.11 pattern. What no checkpoint
had was the incumbent's will to END games: 31/48 against 48/48,
37% censored probes against 16%, factorial censoring 10x the
reference. The incumbent's decisiveness is not one league's product
— it is consolidated across every era since 0.7, and a single
campaign could not re-derive it from scratch. The next campaign
should start from a parent that has it: the frozen artifact's float
lineage bridged forward, however stale its economy senses, rather
than a fresh prior.

**The prime seat-sided game is a standing study.** One
deterministic greedy game per orientation, decided by the seat,
present from update 25, immune to every dial and both league
stages. It is the blunderless-determinism residual class wearing a
new costume, and it caps every future cup sweep at 150/300 on that
rung until someone finds what the losing chair does differently.

**The loans stay on loan, now with the price of the attempt on the
tag**: Expert's outright sweep bar (ceiling 70/80 across the whole
cadence grid) and the duel per-seat floor ([13,9] at 0.13 — a sim
fact no artifact can repay). And the bands: Stage D's duration
badges landed from the FROZEN artifact's fresh pace-sweep, because
the badge waited on the campaign to settle which artifact ships —
it did, just not the way the runbook expected.
