## 2026-07-22 — correction: the RPC split is not bit-identical to serial

Codex review caught an overstatement in the Phase B note. The pipelined
rollout computes every job's actions before any reply is collected,
while the serial loop interleaved episode resets (which consume the
shared seeded generator) between jobs' blunder draws. On steps where an
earlier job's episode ends, later jobs' blunder rolls therefore differ
from what the serial code would have drawn: the refactor changed the
seeded trajectory stream at episode boundaries. What holds: the NEW
order is itself fully deterministic (same seed, same run, every time),
the ordering test pins it, and RL statistics are indifferent to which
correlated noise stream you get. Cross-refactor reproduction of old
runs was the only casualty, and no promoted artifact depends on it.

## 2026-07-22 — Phase G core: real artillery shells

Shells are sim entities now: fixed launch/impact points plus an
arrival tick — no per-tick kinematics, presentation interpolates the
flight (SHELL_SPEED 0.30 tiles/tick; a full-range Bombard lob flies
~32 ticks). Launch replaces the instant PendingHit for projectile
weapons; arrivals join the buffered volley against the start-of-tick
world; the direct hit is distance-to-footprint (an aimed shell lands
on the closest EDGE point, whose exact coordinate floors into the
neighboring tile — tile containment made six-gun sieges deal zero,
caught by a timing-out victory test); units take splash only,
buildings direct only; the fire gate cleared at launch and a shell in
flight chooses nothing, including dying with its gun.

Test-migration lessons, all from real flight time exposing latent
geometry: victims parked inside their own aggro of the gun charge it
mid-flight (the 2-tick instant tests never saw it); a scuttler spotter
4.5 tiles from an enemy foundry marches on it and starts a border war;
pacifist spotters (harvesters) are the honest test instrument. Hashes
re-blessed twice (shell landing + direct-hit fix); goldens never
moved (classic-bot fixture games field no artillery).

## 2026-07-22 — G5: movement parity-alternation, measured and rejected

The 0.8 candidate experiment (parity-alternate movement::run like the
brain loop) finally ran, against post-shell baselines. The shells had
already done most of the work the hypothesis wanted: Expert 2v2 Open
Quarry went from 12/12 thirty-k draws (0.8) to 8/12, and the Twin
Forges decisive lean from 12-2 west to 6-4 — dodgeable artillery
breaks symmetric standoffs on its own. On top of that, parity
movement changed NOTHING measurable: identical 4/12 decisive on Open
Quarry and an identical 6-4-2 Twin Forges split (individual games
differ; aggregates don't improve). Reverted — a hash-moving change
needs a measured win, and this one has none left to claim. The
residual Expert stalls remain bounded to bot-vs-bot spectacles;
Medium stays decisive.

## 2026-07-22 — J1: gym v4 schema freeze

Frozen before any retrain compute burns. Decisions, with rationale:

1. **Relative oriented coordinates, 0-1000.** Every positional
   feature (home, enemy site, last-seen, nearest blip, nearest
   wreck) becomes `oriented_x * 1000 / (map_w - 1)` (same for y).
   The v3 scales were fixed constants (x/48, y/32 — the old maximum
   map), which Ferric Reach (72x44) and Parallel Works (84x52) blow
   straight past: a policy fed >1.0 coordinates is extrapolating.
   Orientation flips compose cleanly: flip-then-scale equals
   1000-minus-scaled, so seat symmetry is untouched.
2. **Map dimensions ride along.** `map_w`, `map_h` (tiles) join the
   vector — relative coordinates hide absolute distance, and march
   timing is an absolute-distance skill. Scale /100 in Python.
3. **Shell observability: two features, fog-safe by construction.**
   `incoming_shells` counts hostile shells whose *impact tile* the
   seat's team currently sees and whose impact lands within 8
   chebyshev of an own building — the same visibility rule the arc
   renderer draws by, so the policy sees exactly what a player sees.
   `my_shells_in_flight` counts own launches (trivially safe).
   Nothing else about a shell is exposed: no shooter, no timer.
4. **Ally-ping: deferred, decision recorded.** Adopting it properly
   needs a recorded command, a freshness observation, a responding
   action, and a curriculum that trains the response — a 1.0-scale
   project. The ally block (units/strength/foundry-hp/distress)
   already covers passive teammate awareness; mixed-ally play is
   explicitly not a 0.9 headline. Revisit if 2v2 becomes one.
5. **Peak-awareness needs no new features.** Placement lowering
   already flows through `can_place` (peaks refuse foundations),
   routing through `route_for` (air A*s around ridges), pushes
   through NoRoute stalls the executive already absorbs. The policy
   reads peaks the way it reads rock: through what its actions can
   and cannot reach. Masks unchanged in shape.

FEATURE_COUNT 59 -> 63 (append-only: map_w, map_h, incoming_shells,
my_shells_in_flight after `faction`). GYM_VERSION 3 -> 4. The
handshake name-assert catches any Rust/Python skew as always.

## 2026-07-22 — K3: pick, and a consolidation lesson paid in one run

Tournament sweep over the v4-main late pool (12 seeds/side, then a
24-seed confirmation on the top two): ckpt-750 sweeps PERFECT —
240/240 across scrapheap/standard/veteran/prime/rusher with zero
draws (CI floor 0.926 per row). ckpt-800 confirms at prime 0.812
with 9 draws; 700 was the inbreeding dip the rusher canary flagged
(0.5). Winner by tournament: **ckpt-750**.

First consolidation attempt (v4-team) collapsed the duel evals to
0/0/0 within 100 updates — root cause: `anchor_coef * 0.995^update`
keys off the ABSOLUTE update clock, so a run resumed at 750 starts
with its anchor already annealed to 2% strength. Team-heavy training
with no effective anchor free-falls, exactly what the 0.8 anchored-
mix rule predicts. Killed at u850, pool deleted. Relaunched as
v4-team2 with the coefficient compensated for the clock
(0.1/0.995^750 = 4.29 nominal — effective 0.1 at consolidation
start, decaying to ~0.03 by u1000) and a lighter team share
(team+team2 = 0.40, scripted share ≈ 0.45). Lesson for the file:
**a resumed anchor must be re-normalized to the resume point.**
