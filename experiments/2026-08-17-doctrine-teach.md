# Doctrine teaching — three designs, one working mechanism

Experiment: can the twelve costless-unused kinds (the viability
oracle's verdict) be taught into the es-1 candidate overnight without
spending its strength or personality? Three designs, measured at each
step by the same instruments: a 12-seed neural cup for strength, the
balance probe pinned to one named profile for facet-keyed census, and
the style gate.

- Design 1, scripted-teacher cloning (`bc.py --resume`, gentle dose):
  Overseer 16/24, Rusher 2/24 — the corpus's whole-playstyle mass
  clobbered the trunk's rush defense — and free play still built no
  skyhooks. The teachers' contexts are not the trunk's contexts.
- Design 2, unconditioned doctrine demos (`doctrine_bc.py` v1): the
  candidate plays its own game while a doctrine forces one lesson per
  seat. Overseer 12-13/24 AND compulsions (bastion 160-221 free picks
  per game). Root cause: the lesson has no input signature, so the net
  averages contradictory labels across episodes into an unconditioned
  mixture. Class weighting amplified it; uniform weighting only
  softened it.
- Design 3, FACET-KEYED doctrine demos (the fix): each lesson taught
  only under canonical profiles whose facet knobs should want it —
  air kinds under air-combined/air-raider, fortification under the
  turtle variants, expansion under the economy lean, sappers under
  siege. The lesson now has the input signature the personality
  system already ships. Census under air-combined: Skyhook 17-33% of
  composition value at full dose, the whole air roster alive.
- Dose response (retrained from the cached corpus): 3 epochs @1e-4 =
  25/48 cup; 1 @1e-4 = 27; 2 @5e-5 = 28; 1 @5e-5 = 32/48 with lessons
  still expressed and the style gate 7/7/7/7. Gentler is stronger;
  the lesson dose survives the gentlest clone tried.
- es-2 (selection polish from the gentlest dose, 3h, 395 generations):
  64 -> 69/96 center, 125 -> 130/192 confirm, style held throughout,
  fun gate FAILED — array reach 15.2% (<25%) and reclaimer reach
  17.7% (<20%). The construction-head lessons crowded out the two
  economy structures the fun gate protects.

Reading: the teaching mechanism is PROVEN (facet-keyed lessons express
under their profiles and survive gentle cloning), but selection alone
cannot pay back cloning damage — es-2 recovered 5 of the ~30 lost
confirm games in three hours where gradient work belongs. The next
cycle is the league/PPO integration this night deliberately deferred:
anchor on the candidate, facet-keyed corpus mixed into rollouts, and
Arrays + Reclaimers added to the lesson schedule under the economy
facets so the fun floors hold.
