# BC prior v9 — first from-scratch demonstration corpus

Experiment: behavior-clone the four v9-surface teacher strategies into
the campaign's starting prior.

- Command: `uv run bc.py --episodes 48 --epochs 30 --arch deep
  --save-every 10 --out runs/prior-v9.pt` (tools/train, driver at
  workspace 0.15.0-beta.2, commit 5f98394).
- Teachers: fortify / industry / combined / pressure over the whole v9
  surface (tier ladders, frame restoration, expansion Foundries,
  upgrade head, airlifts), tier wants scrap-gated. Opponent: the
  Rust-side Overseer on every uncontrolled seat. Slate: the shipped
  two-seat roster (includes severance, subsidence, the-deep-cut).
- Preceding smokes: 3-episode pre-v9 teacher corpus had zero new-verb
  labels (head2 100% NoUpgrade); 4-episode v9 corpus showed labels on
  every head incl. BUILD_EXTRACTOR/CRUCIBLE/AIRWORKS and UPGRADE, with
  teacher wins 2/4 vs the Overseer.

Result: 77,404 samples across 48 episodes; teacher won 21/48 against
the Overseer. Cloning loss 1.13 -> 0.040 over 30 epochs (deep arch).
Corpus carries labels on every head including the v9 verbs (tier
trains, Airworks/Crucible/Extractor/Foundry builds, upgrades,
airlifts). Checkpoint: tools/train/runs/prior-v9.pt (+ epoch-numbered
saves every 10). Next: gym evaluation of the prior vs the Overseer and
a recorded-episode eyeball, then the league from this initialization.
