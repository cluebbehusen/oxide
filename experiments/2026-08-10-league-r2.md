# League r2 — tree-seeded structure bonus (annealed)

Experiment: continue from r1's ckpt-00300 with the structure bonus
seeding the 0.15 tree rungs (airworks, crucible, extractor, foundry)
at the 0.02 per-kind cap plus --mix-bonus 0.02, map mix tilted grand,
prior-v9 anchor constant. 600 updates planned, default (full-span)
anneal on the shaped bonuses.

Result (stopped deliberately at phase-update 200 of 600): the bonus
works exactly as long as it is strong, and the anneal takes it away.
Tier-2/3 combat share 0.087 -> 0.102 (peak, phase-update 100) ->
0.084 -> 0.076 as the schedule decayed; entropy 2.59 -> 2.49;
extractor tenure 0.033 -> 0.024. Decisiveness stayed healthy
(21-27/62 with the rest mostly active long wars). Conclusion: with
the per-kind bonus already at its cap, the lever is the SCHEDULE —
hold the shaping constant instead of annealing it, and continue from
the tier peak (r2's ckpt-00400) rather than the decayed tail.
