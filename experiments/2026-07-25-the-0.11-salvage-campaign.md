confirm): every candidate lost something the parent kept. ckpt-02600
was the only rusher-perfect member (60/60) but takes prime just
39/60; ckpt-02500/02700 sweep prime but sit at 50% vs the rusher

And the verb never took: greedy full-skill probe of ckpt-02600 over

sampling noise), while the composition drifted scuttler-forward and
TrainHarvester vanished from the greedy histogram. A 1500-update
anchored self-play run from a converged parent bought nothing and
paid sharpness — the 0.10 shelf-life lesson, replayed.

Round 2 (salvage011b): a SHORT consolidation resumed FROM the parent
and anchored TO the parent at coef 0.1 decay 1.0 (strength held by

per the "advantageous AND chosen" rule.

## Training-contract corrections (2026-07-26)

Found in review, with direct experiment impact: terminal frames
installed the true final observation but never priced its potential
delta, so a salvage (or an army loss) landing on the terminal cadence
escaped the v5 anti-salvage building-value shaping — future league
runs would have under-priced final-step liquidation. The delta is now
priced (a dead lane's frozen view prices a zero delta by
construction, so padding is unaffected), pinned by a league test. And
OBSERVATION_VERSION moved 4 -> 5 for the UnitObs `salvaging` field,
so recorded v4 observations refuse cleanly instead of failing
mid-parse.

## The flipped-faction probe (2026-07-26)

Launch-time retinting made every seat's faction freely choosable,
raising the question whether the policy holds strength from
faction x orientation combinations absent from training (authored
maps deal even seats Ferrous; a retrain was pre-authorized if the
answer came back no).

The probe (skirmish, 40k-tick cap, dealt personalities, 24
seeds/config): NO generalization collapse — Cupric wins 16/24 from
the untrained seat 0 vs 18/24 from its trained seat 1 at Expert, and
undecided counts are no worse flipped (3 vs 5 Expert, 8 vs 13
Medium). RETRAIN NOT NEEDED; the faction knob generalizes across
orientations. The first identical-dial probe was a trap worth
recording: Expert with a fixed aggression knob is deterministic, so
24 seeds replay ONE game — vary the dealt personalities or the sweep
is a coin reported 24 times.

Side findings, recorded rather than blamed on the feature: (1) the
0.11 artifact leans Cupric on skirmish (34-6 Expert aggregate across
seat-swapped pairs — present in the convention configuration, so in
the shipped game already; the artifact-specific lean class). (2)
Same-faction mirrors are now player-reachable: the Cupric mirror is
12/12 decisive by 120k ticks (6-6 across seats), the Ferrous mirror
10/12 with two games grinding past 100 game-minutes — the
near-deterministic bot-vs-bot mirror artifact, broken by any human
in the match.
