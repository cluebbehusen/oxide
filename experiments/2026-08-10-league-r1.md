# League r1 — first from-scratch PPO phase off the v9 prior

Experiment: PPO league from `runs/prior-v9.pt` (BC on the v9-surface
teachers, 77,404 samples), Overseer-anchored, across the new roster
and generator.

- Command: `uv run league.py --name r1-prior-league --initialize-from
  runs/prior-v9.pt --anchor runs/prior-v9.pt --collection episodes
  --updates 300 --mix "self=0.35,past=0.10,overseer=0.20,rusher=0.10,
  ffa=0.10,team=0.15" --map-mix "fixed=.30,random=.30,grand=.40"
  --probe-every 25` (workspace 0.15.0-beta.2; league.py carried one
  live v9 fix first: the PPO buffer still reshaped plans as three
  heads — `act_b.reshape(-1, len(ACTION_HEADS))` now).
- Early telemetry (updates 1-18): teching retained in ~10/11 episodes
  per update under the constant anchor; turret/array/reclaimer builds,
  repairs, and salvages sampling; all six opponent kinds realized;
  named-profile curriculum dealing styles, variants, roles, and
  execution tiers; 580-1372 decisions/s. Noted: faction_mix logs
  fc=1.0 (the historical duel default) — revisit whether the league
  should also deal ff/cf/cc.

Result: 300 updates complete. Final eval: 45% vs the Overseer, 55% vs
the rusher. Decisiveness and finish latency improved through the phase
(latency median ~6,365 early -> ~4,183-4,777 late); fight rhythm held
(~31-43 windows/match); of 44 capped probe matches at the end, 29 were
ALIVE long wars by activity and fight share (good, per the
long-matches-are-good-when-dynamic principle) and 15 stalled. The
phase's clear loss: escalation decayed — tier-2/3 combat share peaked
at 0.141 (update 100) and slid to 0.083 by 300; extractor tenure never
developed (0.043 -> 0.024); entropy 2.65 -> 2.59. The win signal alone
does not pay for the climb, even under the constant anchor. A recorded
eyeball episode at update 100 showed a coherent losing game vs the
Overseer (built, teched, fought, conceded honestly through recovery).

Follow-up (r2): seed the 0.15 tree rungs (airworks, crucible,
extractor, foundry) into --structure-bonus, enable --mix-bonus, tilt
the map mix toward grand, and continue 600 updates from ckpt-00300
with the prior-v9 anchor held constant.
