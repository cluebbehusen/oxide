# The 0.13 old world (2026-07-28)

The BEFORE side. The 0.13 plan puts a sim batch (Phase 4) between
today's rules and the retraining campaign: a roster rebalance, wreck
decay and harvester-retarget, buildings dropping out of the direct-fire
cover rule, the stacked-build fix. Every one of those moves hashes, and
after they land nobody can measure the old world again. So this file is
the old world, measured — six instruments run at the last commit before
the batch, with the invocations verbatim so the same slate re-runs
after the bless and the two columns can be read side by side.

Nothing here is a verdict on a change. It is the reference point.

Provenance: branch `0.13.0` at `814683a`, workspace 0.13.0, release
build, 10-core machine. Every bot number is the embedded ladder
artifact, digest `7c733a948eb315ff`. All levels Medium unless the
instrument's job is the ladder itself. Whole slate: about three
minutes of wall clock, the factorial carrying 1:37 of it.

Three readings the table forces:

- **The seat bundle carries nothing.** Spawn order, command order and
  RNG stream are all inside noise, and the command factor's two levels
  produced *identical* victory counts (790/727 both ways) — the
  suspicion that started this instrument is dead.
- **Geometry is not neutral.** 45.0% vs 59.1% for the same seat played
  from the authored map and its 180-degree rotation, intervals nowhere
  near overlapping: the east half is worth roughly 14 points on a map
  that is geometrically its own mirror. Second-largest effect in the
  design, an order of magnitude under the faction effect, and NOT
  diagnosed here. Recorded.
- **Ferrous mirrors grind.** FF median 7,392 and p75 15,130 against
  CC's 5,409 / 5,937 — a Ferrous-only game takes ~37% longer at the
  median and has a long tail that the Cupric mirror does not.

## The arena — the roster gap under controlled conditions

`matchup`, arena default seed 42, 8k cap, both seats wearing one
roster label (the arena runs no economy, so the label selects no
stat). One seed, two legs; no seed flag exists, so read these as
sharp point measurements, not distributions.

**Air-to-ground at par cost, against a common 1,440-scrap Sentinel
line** (Sentinel's skyward sidearm makes it a real fight):

| A (1440 scrap) | A mean survivor | hp-weighted | legs |
|---|---|---|---|
| buzzard:9 | 1200 | 1029 | wipe at 341 / 356 ticks |
| darter:16 | 1350 | 1163 | wipe at 205 / 194 ticks |

The Darter clears the same line **40% faster** and comes out one
Buzzard's worth of value ahead. This is the roster gap in a box.

**Anti-air at par cost, against a common wing** — and this one
reverses the paper:

| A (1440 scrap) | vs buzzard:9 | vs darter:16 |
|---|---|---|
| flakhound:16 | 1350 (hp-w 1218), 171/174 ticks | **1440** (hp-w 1314), 116/115 ticks |
| stinger:32 | 1260 (hp-w 1223), 164/143 ticks | 1350 (hp-w 1242), 119/108 ticks |

The Flakhound wins on residual value against *both* wings, losing zero
units against Darters where the Stinger loses two. The scoping's
"Stinger is 1.56x Flakhound's dps-per-cost" is a stats-table ratio the
arena does not honor: 120 hp and a 1.2 splash radius price in, and
dps-per-cost does not see them.

**Air superiority head to head** (the only same-role pair that can
shoot each other), wisp:11 vs talon:7 at 770 each: leg one B 220-0,
leg two A 280-0 — **paired mean A 140 / B 110, verdict flips on
swap.** Par within the instrument's resolution.

**Siege, with the garrison pitch knob** — sentinel:12 (1,080) against
a turret:6 garrison (600), the plan's shape, at three spacings:

| pitch | result | A / B mean survivor |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | unresolved (no-progress, both legs) | 270 / 250 |
| 2 | **B wins outright**, A wiped both legs | 0 / 300 |
| 1 | **A wins outright**, B wiped both legs | 360 / 0 |

Non-monotone, and the finding is the middle row. A turret block only
blinds itself when its towers are *fully adjacent*: at pitch 1 the
buildings-as-cover rule guts the defense and 1,080 scrap of Sentinels
walks through 600 scrap of turrets; at pitch 2 the same six turrets
wipe the same twelve Sentinels. The plan's "a dense turret block loses
outright" reproduces at pitch 1 and only at pitch 1. Whatever Phase 4
does to the cover rule, this row is the one to re-run — it is the
before-picture of the exact interaction being changed.

## The fun gate

`fun_gate.py` against the shipped artifact, its own defaults (its
internal probe: 2 seeds, 20k cap):

```
judging 37 decided of 50 matches (104 seats)
entropy 2.26 bits · per-seat p10 1.52 bits · shares sentinel 28.5%,
scuttler 24.3%, harvester 23.0%, darter 17.2%, lancer 5.9%,
buzzard 0.6%, stinger 0.5%
fun gate: open
```

**Verdict: open.** Entropy 2.26 against the 1.8 floor; tech kinds
carry 48.5% of army value against the 25% floor; the fattest tech kind
is Scuttler at 24.3% against the 3% floor. Its independent 50-match
sample reproduces the 150-match probe's entropy (2.26 vs 2.26) and p10
(1.52 vs 1.50), which is the cheapest available check that the
composition record is not seed-lucky.

## The reading

**The roster gap is one slot, not a roster.** The factorial puts
Ferrous at 21.7% [19.7, 23.9] of mixed-roster victories and clears
every engine-side seat advantage of suspicion. The arena localizes it:
Darter over Buzzard at par cost on both value and clock; Wisp/Talon at
par with the verdict flipping on the seat swap; Flakhound *ahead of*
Stinger against either wing. The bot's own purchases agree —
darter 17.4% of army value against buzzard 0.8%, Cupric's top kind
Darter at 35% while Ferrous's is Scuttler at 38% with no air option
worth the scrap, and a Ferrous cohort entropy of 1.93 against Cupric's
2.16. The plan's ordering (Buzzard first, then smaller
Flakhound/Talon nudges) is half-confirmed: **Buzzard first is
measured; the Flakhound nudge is unsupported by the arena and should
not ship on the dps-per-cost argument alone.**

**Composition p10 sits at 1.54 slate-wide, 1.50 in the decided
cohort**, with a per-map floor that is entirely explained by short
maps and one long one: Scrapyard Brawl 0.96, Long Haul 1.07, Open
Circuit 1.22, Oxide Flats 1.23. Everything else clears 1.39. Three
kinds (Bombard, Talon, Wisp) and four structures (Reclaimer, Array,
Bastion, Flak Turret) are dead weight in the current policy's hands —
the campaign's in-loop composition sampling has its baseline.

**Which maps run long**: the vast and large classes, 17-18 minutes at
the median and past 26 in the tail; standard lands 4:30-8:30, quick
under 4:20. Two labels disagree with their measurements (Long Haul
fast, Open Circuit wide), which is a map-authoring note, not a bug.

**What to re-run after the Phase 4 bless**, in order of expected
movement: the siege pitch table (the cover rule is the thing being
changed), the arena's air-to-ground pair (the roster rebalance is
aimed there), the factorial (the tolerance gate the roster fix must
pass), the composition record (wreck decay and retarget radius change
what an army is made of), then pace and yardstick to see what the
batch cost the ladder. The commands above are the whole protocol.
