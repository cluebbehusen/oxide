a bless window, retrain under the fun gates.

## 0.10 G — campaign rounds 2-3: the techless-lineage root cause

so did its BC prior. Root cause found in bc.py: NEITHER teacher ever

actions, and every policy in the lineage (the shipped 0.9 artifact

balance story and the learner story meet here: the meta was spam all
the way down to the imitation data.

Second bug in the same lesson: after adding the fab branch, action 9
STILL never fired — sentinel training at 75 a pop ate the bank before
it reached the Fabricator price. The teachers now cap their pre-tech
line (3-4 sentinels) and save. Recording telemetry (action counts)
added to bc.py so an absent action can never hide again.

bc-010c prior: fields darter/stinger/scuttler in real play, entropy
1.18 bits (from 0.98). Round 3 league runs from it.

## 0.10 G — round 3 verdict, round 4 design (2026-07-23)

Round 3 (fresh league from the teching bc-010c prior, standard 0.995
anchor decay, 1200 updates): weaker than round 1's resumed lineage
across every tier (nothing beats Prime), and by ckpt-01200 the tech is

caps how far past the teachers the policy can drift; if the candidate
techs but plateaus at ~Veteran strength, promotion becomes a judgment
call between fun (gate) and raw strength (round-1's spam sweep),
recorded for review.

If round 4 also fails the gate, the honest next lever is the game
itself: spam may simply be optimal under current numbers even at long
horizons — then stats.rs moves again (sentinel cost up, or advanced
units cheaper) until the incentive and the gate agree.

## THE CAMPAIGN CONCLUDES: the fun full-tech bot ships (b71bb2c)

Round 10's ckpt-00875 is embedded as the ladder artifact. The final
three findings, each load-bearing:

1. THE ARTIFACT (round 10, clean experiment): 300/300 tournament
   sweep, 240/240 as the quantized bot, 2.20 bits / 39% tech at full
   skill, and tech share that ROSE after the anneal (44% tail).

2. THE MISTAKE MODEL was the last confound: wrong-action blunders
   (even the "plausible second-best" kind) spend the Fabricator fund
   mid-save, so EVERY degraded level rationally spammed — measured
   1.07-1.13 bits at all sub-Expert operating points, and the
   decoupling probe pinned it on blunders, not conditioning
   (cond-1000 + 60 permille wrong actions -> 1.11 bits). Hesitation
   blunders (the commander dithers, the window passes) fix it:
   2.15-2.40 bits at every rung. SHIPPED DEFAULT: 2.30 bits.

3. THE LADDER re-metered: head-to-head mirrors stopped ordering
   because PATIENCE WINS under the new balance (slower thinkers
   turtle into tech advantages — Easy beat Medium head-to-head!).
   Difficulty is now hesitation (35/19/7.5/0 percent) x cadence
   (56/36/24/16 ticks), gated against the scripted yardsticks:
   51 < 72 < 76 < 80 of 80, Expert perfect. neural_ladder un-ignored
   on a prime-weighted pinned slate.

Instruments that did the work: matchup (par-cost condemnation of the

decomposition), the grand curriculum, and the fun gate itself.
