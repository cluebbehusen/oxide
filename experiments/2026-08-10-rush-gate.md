# Closing the rush exploit — r6/r7/r8

The candidate (r4-final) passed everything but the rush canary:
neural-cup 85% vs the Overseer, 33% vs the Rusher.

- r6 (rusher mix 0.30, 200 updates): traded Overseer strength (85%
  -> 73%) for a marginal rush gain (33% -> 40%). Rejected.
- Diagnosis 1 (recorded episodes): against the all-in the candidate
  opens with ~2 unit purchases and dies on an identical clock every
  seed — it never screens. Training beat its rusher 70-75% while the
  cup said 33%: the curriculum dealt mixed execution profiles, so the
  learner practiced against slow-motion rushes; the cup runs expert
  cadence. Fix: rusher jobs pin every seat to expert execution
  (e12b2a5).
- r7 (gate-tempo rush, 300 updates): Overseer held at 75%; cup rusher
  STILL 33% — but the by_seat split exposed the real hole: as Ferrous
  17/30, as Cupric 3/30. The league's faction_mix was fc-only since
  r1 (flagged in its notes), so "Cupric" and "seat 1" were perfectly
  confounded all campaign and the Cupric-conditioned style never
  learned the fast defense.
- r8: full faction deal (ff/fc/cf/cc at .25 each) from r7-final,
  rush emphasis retained, 250 updates. Result: the confound was the
  hole. Cup 88% vs the Overseer (campaign best to that point) and the
  rusher closed 33% -> 62% with the seat split healed (Cupric now the
  STRONGER defending side). Re-measured under the beta.4 Array
  rebalance: 83% / 62% (17F/20C) — the defense is robust to the
  economy patch. The exploit is closed; remaining candidate selection
  weighs rush defense against Overseer strength across r8/r9/r10
  (see the structure-reach and league-r10 logs).
