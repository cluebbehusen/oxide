# 0.7 — the learned-bot campaign (2026-07-20/21)

> Part of the [experiment log](README.md): the working lab notebook
> of Oxide's bot-training and balance campaigns, kept as it was
> written. Newest entries at the bottom.

All win rates are seat-swapped (each seed played from both
seats) unless noted. "Cup" = native Rust tournament of the quantized
artifact (`oxide-driver neural-cup`); "tournament" = torch-side eval
(`tools/train/tournament.py`).

## Setup (constants across experiments)

- Gym: 32 integer features (fog-honest, seat-oriented, incl. fog
  memory), 11 masked macro actions, decisions every 16 ticks, terminal
  reward ±1 (draw −0.3), own-material potential shaping, map: skirmish.
- Network input = 32 gym features + 2 conditioning knobs (skill,
  aggression; /1000).
- League roles per rollout: 4× self-play, 2× past checkpoint, 1×
  scripted tier (random), 1× scripted rusher. PPO: lr 1e-4, γ 0.999,
  GAE 0.95, clip 0.2, KL-stop 0.03, value warm-up 15 updates.
- BC teachers: rusher (4 harvesters, push at 5) and turtle (5
  harvesters, 3 turrets, push at 10) — both beat Scrapheap alone.

**23:1x — architecture sweep launched**: three concurrent conditioned

**23:4x — procedural maps (an overnight directive)**: mapgen.py
generates 180°-symmetric scenarios (mirrored rock blobs, home + center
scrap, mirror-ordered spawns) — 30/30 build and run on the first try.
Wired in: `tournament.py --random-maps N` and `league.py --maps random`
(fresh map per training episode).

First honest generalization measurement (league7 wide @625, 15 unseen
maps × both seats):

| Opponent | wins | draws | losses | rate | median ticks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Veteran | 21 | 9 | 0 | 70% | 9788 |
| Prime | 10 | 10 | 10 | 33% | 6059 |

vs 100% sweeps on skirmish → **part of the strength is map-learned**.
Zero losses to Veteran but 30% stalls: it doesn't lose on strange
terrain, it gets passive. The generalist run (train on random maps)
is queued for the arch winner.

**00:1x — FFA groundwork (the overnight asks)**: mapgen grows
4-player maps (four-fold symmetry, 15/15 build); the gym server now
runs n-player episodes (any control subset, scripted brains fill the
rest, and a controlled seat's episode ends at its elimination — a loss,
not a spectate-to-cap draw). League gains an `ffa` role (learner seat
rotates 0-3, three scripted opponents on a generated 4p map);
tournament gains `--ffa-maps N`.

FFA baseline of the duel-trained model (1-of-4, 8 maps, vs Standard /
Veteran fills): **1/8 wins both — below the 25% chance line** — with
5-6 stall-draws. It survives multi-front games but cannot win them:
duel strategy assumes one enemy site. This is the training gap the ffa
role addresses in the generalist run.

**Teams (design sketch, NOT built — real sim surgery, 0.8-sized)**:
the sim has no alliance concept; teams need (a) scenario alliance
groups, (b) targeting/auto-acquire/retaliation treating allies as
non-hostile, (c) shared victory (last *team* standing), (d) probably
shared vision, all hash-moving with a big test surface. Training a
good teammate then falls out of the same league machinery: control two
allied seats (one learner one scripted ally or both learner), shared
terminal reward, maybe an assist-shaping term. The n-player gym work
tonight is the direct foundation.

**0x:xx — league8-mlp (128×2) complete, pool swept** (4 seeds × both
seats vs Veteran/Prime per ckpt): strong early — ckpt-200: 1.00 vet /

prime with stalls). Final weights: 0/0. Small-net verdict: **capacity

**0x:xx — league9-deep (384×3) complete, pool swept** (8 games per
