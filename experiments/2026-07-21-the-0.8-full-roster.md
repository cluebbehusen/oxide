# 0.8 campaign — gym v3 and the full-roster retrain

> Part of the [experiment log](README.md): the working lab notebook
> of Oxide's bot-training and balance campaigns, kept as it was
> written. Newest entries at the bottom.

The world grew: air domain, weapons matrix (splash, indirect, the fire
gate), 7 new unit kinds behind a faction gate, 4 new buildings, wreck
salvage, repair, radar blips, and teams. The v2 net (32 features, 11
actions, 2 knobs) cannot express any of it. This section logs the v3
contract and every run.

## Gym v3 contract (design, pre-implementation)

Feature count target: ~56. Layout keeps v2's first 32 indices where the
meaning is unchanged (BC transfer from recorded v2 data is impossible
anyway — the teachers changed — but stable prefixes keep SCALES review
sane). New features, all integers, all fog-honest:

- Role counts, mine: bombards, anti-air, air-ground wings, air-air
  wings (4)
- Role counts, known enemy: same four, live-sight only (4)
- My AA coverage: flak turrets built, sentinels (the sidearm floor) (2)
- Air strength mine / known enemy air strength (strength_vs Air) (2)
- Blips: count, nearest blip oriented x, y, or -1 (3)
- Wrecks: known count, total known value, nearest oriented x, y (4)
- Damaged own buildings: count, total hp deficit (2)
- Reclaimers built + my reclaimer income flag (1)
- Ally block: ally unit count, ally strength/100, ally foundry hp,
  nearest ally foundry oriented x, y, ally-in-distress flag (6)
- Faction (0 ferrous / 1 cupric) (1)
  -> 32 + 29 = 61… trim to keep the head tight; cut ally foundry x, y
  (distress flag suffices) and reclaimer income flag => 58. Final count
  fixed at implementation; FEATURE_NAMES const emitted in the gym hello
  and asserted Python-side so Rust/Python index skew is structurally
  impossible.

Action count target: 20, role-indexed (faction resolves the kind):
  0 Idle, 1 TrainHarvester, 2 TrainSentinel, 3 TrainScuttler,
  4 TrainLancer, 5 TrainBombard, 6 TrainAntiAir, 7 TrainAirGround,
  8 TrainAirAir, 9 BuildFabricator, 10 BuildTurret, 11 BuildFlak,
  12 BuildBastion, 13 BuildArray, 14 BuildReclaimer, 15 Repair,
  16 AirRaid, 17 FormArmy, 18 Push, 19 Recall, 20 Scout => 21.
  (v2's 11 actions keep their relative order where meanings survive.)

Conditioning: 3 knobs — skill, aggression, faction*1000. CONDITION_DIMS
= 3 through bc/league/export/neural; QuantNet is already parametric.

Masks stay trained legality (0.7 lesson: widening a mask feeds untrained
logits to the blunder picker); lowering stays total (Repair with no
patient, AirRaid with no wings -> no-op commands, absorbed).

## Campaign sequencing

1. E1: Rust gym v3 + FEATURE_NAMES + neural knobs=3 + bridge protocol.
2. Bridge: BC-only artifact from the new tiers immediately after the
   version bump — the crate must never sit broken. Ladder-monotonicity
   test #[ignore]d until the final artifact.
3. BC teachers: Veteran + Prime across both factions, 1v1 and 2v2 maps,
   rusher + turtle style teachers re-recorded under the new doctrine.
4. League: mix adds team=0.15 (two learners, one team, shared policy,
   distinct conditioning); ffa stays 0.15-0.25; random maps include 2v2.
   Reward hygiene: no shaping on blips/known-enemy (information
   artifacts); own-material potential only, wreck income counts by
   construction (it lands in the bank).
5. Tournament gate: seat-swapped AND faction-swapped pairs; neural-cup
   extended with a 2v2 bracket + anti-air sanity opponent (mass wings
   vs the candidate's ground army — it must buy AA, not ignore the sky).
6. Calibrate Level::skill() thresholds on the final artifact; un-ignore
   the ladder test; re-bless fixtures (final artifact = final hashes).

## 2026-07-22 — post-release review: padded team lanes leaked gradients

Codex review found that team/team2 rollouts fed a dead learner's
frozen-view padding rows into the PPO update (real actions sampled
against a stale observation, zero reward). league.py now marks rows
collected while the seat is dead and masks them out of the flattened
update batch; GAE still runs over the full rectangle so the episode's
team payoff discounts back through the padding to the live prefix.

The shipped 0.8 artifact (v3e-550) was trained WITH the leak. It
stands as gated — 300/300 main, ladder monotone, externally measured —
and the leak plausibly acted as mild regularization noise rather than
bias (rewards on padded rows were zero, advantages near zero after
value convergence). The mask is a correctness fix for future runs;
any 0.9 retrain gets it for free. Worth one A/B lane if team play
becomes a headline mode: same seed league, mask on vs off, compare
the 2v2 bracket.

## 2026-07-22 — team2 episodes were truncated at learner death

PR review found the gym ended an episode when no *controlled* seat held
a Foundry. In team2 (scripted ally) the learner is the only controlled
seat, so its own death ended the episode with no winner: every
self-sacrifice scored as a loss, and the learner never saw games its
ally went on to win. Liveness is now team-scoped (any controlled seat's
team holds a Foundry); dead learners still drop from the frame's seats
and ride the existing padding to the true team outcome.

The 0.8 artifact's mixed-ally bracket (25-31%) was trained AND measured
under the truncation. The measurement stands (external, bracket-based);
the training signal it grew from was biased against sacrifice plays.
Combined with the padded-row gradient mask, the next team-focused run
starts from a meaningfully cleaner signal — re-baseline the bracket
before crediting any algorithmic change.
