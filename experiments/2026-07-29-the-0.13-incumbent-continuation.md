# The 0.13 incumbent-continuation campaign

## Question

Can the decisive shipped v6 actor be recovered as a trainable parent, taught the
new repair verbs, and made visibly more varied without repeating the
from-scratch campaign's stalls and faction skew?

Artifact under test:

- Shipped ladder digest: `7e6f1303875b983e`
- Gym contract: v6, 65 raw features plus 3 conditioning inputs, 24 actions
- Architecture: three 384-wide tanh layers with a Q12 policy head

## Preflight findings

The failed campaign exposed training-process problems that must be removed before
another long run:

- The per-decision personality reward could total `+6.25` over a capped match,
  versus `+1` for a win. It rewarded keeping the game alive in the preferred
  posture more than ending it.
- `value_only=True` still updated the actor's shared trunk. A reconstructed
  critic could therefore damage the recovered actor during its supposed warmup.
- Warmup used the checkpoint's absolute update, so a resumed parent skipped it.
- Training paired Ferrous with even seats and Cupric with odd seats. It never
  crossed faction with physical seat, matching the rejected candidate's faction
  failure.
- `--maps grand` abandoned the small and standard maps used by the promotion
  slate. The long-map tech curriculum also discarded the incumbent's short-map
  finishing behavior.
- The fun gate checked cost-weighted mean composition, but not the reported
  per-seat floor or the share of matches decided. Cheap-unit body spam could
  look healthy when a few expensive specialists carried the value mix.

The Q12 policy is recoverable even though its critic and optimizer were not
exported. Direct dequantization by `coefficient / 4096` followed by export
reproduced every policy tensor exactly. A probe over 2,233 real decisions found
zero greedy-action disagreements between the recovered float actor and the
integer runtime.

## Parent recovery

```sh
cd tools/train

  --weights ../../sim/src/bot/ladder_weights.json \

uv run dequantize.py \
  --weights ../../sim/src/bot/ladder_weights.json \

  --unfloor-actions 22,23
uv run export.py \
  --ckpt runs/incumbent-v6-exact.pt \
  --out runs/incumbent-v6-roundtrip.json
```

Result:

- The exact parent's semantic JSON was identical to
  `ladder_weights.json` after key-order normalization.
- Both recovered checkpoints carry a deterministic all-zero value head.
- The trainable parent differs only in actions 22 and 23: their all-zero
  policy rows remain zero and their Q12 bridge bias moves from `-32768`
  (`-8.0`) to `0`. No trained row is erased.

## Balance hypothesis check

Before changing unit stats, the current matchup arena tested whether absent
Turrets were simply weak:

```text
scuttler:10 (400 scrap) vs turret:4 (400 scrap)
  turrets won both seat-swapped legs, retaining 200 scrap

sentinel:12 (1080 scrap) vs turret:6 (600 scrap), dense pitch
  turrets won both legs and eliminated every attacker, retaining 250 scrap mean
```

This does not measure the strategic opportunity cost of constructing defenses,
but it rejects the simple claim that Turrets are tactically underpowered. Their
low usage is treated as a policy/curriculum problem until an economy-level
counterfactual says otherwise.

The body-time lens confirms the playtest report: Scuttlers are under one third
of army value but account for over two fifths of integrated unit presence. The
promotion gate now keeps the incumbent's decisiveness and value-diversity floors
while setting deliberately aspirational body-time floors:

- at least 70% of matches decided
- cost-weighted per-seat p10 at least 1.45 bits
- body-time entropy at least 2.05 bits
- body-time per-seat p10 at least 1.45 bits
- largest per-seat body-time share p90 at most 60%
- leading unit's mean body-time share at most 40%

The shipped artifact fails only the four new body-time checks. A replacement
must therefore make the visible composition better, not merely preserve an
already-unsatisfying baseline.

This is an exact capability baseline, not a reward target. The campaign should

reachable and useful under a deliberately favorable state.

## Campaign plan

1. Recover two float parents from the shipped Q12 artifact: one exact anchor and
   one trainable parent with RepairUnit and BuildRepairBay lifted from their
   unreachable output floors.
2. Warm the new zero critic without moving any actor parameter.
3. Continue from the incumbent under a fixed KL anchor, no per-step style
   reward, crossed faction/seat assignments, and a fixed/random/grand map mix.
4. Run a natural continuation first. Run repair seeding only as a controlled
   comparison, rewarding successful effects rather than action selection.
5. Select checkpoints by decisiveness, faction parity, body and value
   composition, repair success, and the existing ladder gates. Do not select by
   recency.

## Natural continuation

The first control run used no repair, structure, mix, or style bonus:

```sh
uv run league.py \
  --name inc013-natural \
  --resume runs/incumbent-v6-trainable.pt \
  --anchor runs/incumbent-v6-trainable.pt \
  --incumbent runs/incumbent-v6-exact.pt \
  --anchor-coef 0.10 --anchor-decay 1.0 --lr 3e-5 \
  --value-warmup 25 --updates 400 --style-coef 0 \
  --map-mix fixed=.25,random=.25,grand=.50 \
  --faction-mix ff=.25,fc=.25,cf=.25,cc=.25 \
  --mix self=.25,past=.10,incumbent=.20,tier=.20,rusher=.10,ffa=.15 \
  --probe-every 50 --probe-seeds 3
```

The standard two-seed gate repeated the result. Update 1350 passed at 43/50
decided, 2.137 body entropy, 1.485 p10, 58.0% dominance p90, and 35.8%
Scuttler body share. Update 1600 also passed the smaller sample but had already
shown a fragile margin on the three-seed slate.

Native Q12 cup results for update 1350 matched the incumbent's ladder profile
exactly: 60/60 against Scrapheap, Standard, and Veteran, 30/60 against Prime,
with zero draws. Its float policy also went 60/60 against the rusher. On twelve
fresh random maps it stayed within two games of the exact parent on Prime and
the rusher, and on the 2v2 team slate it improved every scripted-tier result.

The capability probe prevents a premature promotion. Updates 1350 through 1650
issued no `RepairUnit` command, completed no Repair Bay, and healed nothing in
all eight favorable cases. Update 1700 completed four Bays and healed four
Cupric cases, but never issued `RepairUnit`, healed no Ferrous case, and failed
four composition gates.

**Verdict:** update 1350 is the natural winner and the parent for a short,
effect-seeded continuation. It is not a shipping candidate because neither v6
repair verb survived greedy quantized play. The later checkpoints show that the
Bay is learnable, but also restate why recency and isolated capability cannot
override the whole gate battery.
