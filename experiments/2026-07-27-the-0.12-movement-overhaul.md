# The 0.12 movement overhaul — the tether and the slide

The second 0.12 campaign, run after the decisiveness work shipped.
Player reports: collision "drag" between moving units (replacing the
fixed jitter), stationed units chasing a passing harvester forever,
and bulk attacks where "only the first layer" could fight. An
eleven-agent adversarial review (three competing designs, two judges,
one adversary; probes and RTS-industry research) preceded
implementation; this note records what the code and the gates then
taught.

## The baselines (movement_lab.rs, before any change)

- Head-on swap, two Sentinels, one row: **frozen forever** at exactly
  0.700 separation (r+r) — 0.000000 tiles over 22,000 ticks. Exact
  arithmetic cancellation: path following closes 2v per tick, the
  radial pair-push returns exactly v to each. Re-clicking the same
  goal is a no-op (order-reissue rule), so a player cannot unstick it.
- Real bot matches, 25 shipped maps: path-following units average
  **82.0% of nominal speed** (skirmish 60.7%, open-circuit 44.2%).
- Overtake +42%; mover through a parked line +45% with a parked idle
  bulldozed 14.2 tiles; a mover vs an anchored worker crawls at 10%.
- Pursuit: no leash anywhere. A stationed Sentinel chased a passing
  harvester **222 tiles** (77% of it blind — the chase reads true
  positions through fog; only the shot is sight-gated), then idled
  where the chase died. One 50-scrap harvester held a six-Sentinel
  picket off its post 99.1% of a match. The shell's only ground verb
  re-acquires within 1 tick, so recall was impossible (1.38 tiles
  moved in 2,000 ticks of live orders).
- Bulk attack (20 scuttlers vs a foundry): 19/20 eventual hitters but
  kill at 513 ticks open / 524 pocketed; peak concurrent contact 15.

## The pursuit tether

Shipped shape: only a STATIONED machine (40 ticks standing idle)
tethers when it self-acquires; anchor = its post. Inside
LEASH_RADIUS 10 it hunts freely (its zone); beyond, each chase tick
spends a 60-tick warm-blood window that only a joined fight (firing
stance or answering a hit) refreshes — a bait that never came in
reach grants none, so the kited guard breaks at the radius line
exactly. Break-offs walk home and stand a 60-tick re-acquire
cooldown; victories stand their ground, still stationed. Player
attacks never tether; `assign` clears the leash unconditionally
(no-op reissue path included — the blood-oath test).

Every clause above was carved by a failing gate, in order:

1. **Tether everything idle** → the scripted tier ladder collapsed to
   a seat-parity coin (Veteran/Standard and Prime/Veteran both 10-10
   wide, seat 0 winning every seed). Root cause was NOT the bounds —
   at LEASH_RADIUS=100 the collapse persisted. It was the victory
   arm's rhythm: re-hunting inside `attack()` (one tick early,
   skipping the idle beat) perturbed scripted battles enough to hand
   the outcome to the sim's residual first-mover micro. Station
   gating + baseline victory rhythm restored 20-0 on every rung.
2. **Walk home after victories** → three fog-honest duel seeds lost
   to the same classic rush at the same tick: each garrison kill
   inserted an oblivious walk-away while the next raider hit the
   foundry. Victories stand their ground.
3. **Flat radius break** → the scripted tiers' wounded-rotation
   became unpunishable (their preservation trick relies on pursuers
   giving up) and Veteran's identity collapsed. Blood drawn buys
   followthrough; distance alone does not.
4. **Patience as a flat chase budget (5 s)** → cut legitimate pursuit
   tails in bot fights and inverted the neural ladder. Patience is
   now the beyond-radius window only; inside the zone the guard
   chases as long as it likes.

After: max drift bounded to the zone (~10 + 60·speed), the picket
recovers its post when the bait leaves, a returning guard answers
fire, and the dancer exploit costs the harvester its life at the
first turnaround through weapon range.

## The lateral slide

`movement::run` returns per-tick displacement; a body that traveled
into a contact corrects along `0.5·away + 0.859375·sideways` (both
exactly representable in Q32.32; squares sum to 0.98828125 < 1 so
COLLISION_MAX_STEP's bound is untouched), side picked toward its own
travel — head-on pairs provably take opposite world sides, and the
rule is 180°-equivariant including the tie. Terrain-rejected slides
DROP the lateral against a head-on partner (reversing re-converges a
corridor pair — the adversary's H1); parked, non-closing, and
stacked bodies keep the pure radial push, which is why
behavior_settle held without a dial moving.

Lab, before → after:

| scenario | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| head-on swap | frozen forever | both arrive at exactly solo (269) |
| overtake | 280 vs 197 solo | 197 (= solo) |
| parked line | 389 vs 269; 14.2-tile shove | 270; 0.95-tile shove |
| group march (8) | 271, 0 stranded | 269, 0 stranded |
| bulk attack (melee) | 19/20 hitters, kill 513 | 20/20, kill 432 |
| bulk attack (pocket) | kill 524 | 20/20, kill 426 |
| bulk attack (ranged) | — | peak contact 20/20, kill 510 |

The ranged result answered the "Halo Wars fan-out" ask outright: no
siege goal-spread mechanism was needed — rear layers slide around the
anchored front line into range on their own. `approach_rect`'s
nearest-four doorstep rotation stays as it was.

## What the overhaul cost, eyes open

Any movement change re-rolls every bot-vs-bot match. Un-ground
movement helps massed pushes most, and the shipped policy trained
under the old physics: the widened yardstick (10 seeds × 4 tiers × 2
seats) reads Easy 39 / Medium 57 / Hard 56 / Expert 60 of 80 — pace
of victory still orders all four rungs strictly with Expert holding
the top count, which is what the gate now asserts. Dialing the slide
softer (lateral 0.6) did NOT recover the old numbers — the counts
whipsaw chaotically at any setting because the 0.12 dial calibration
was fitted to the old physics — so the slide ships at its
measured-best feel and the sweep bar returns with the next training
campaign, which trains under the new movement (the correct order:
training first would have mistrained). Decisiveness carried over
(sweep 48/48 decided before and after, medians 5666 → 5541; the 8-40
Cupric lean predates the overhaul). Bench: 7,122 ticks/s on the
500-unit battle — no perf story.

The instrument evolutions this forced, each measured before edited:
the yardstick gained pace-of-victory when the pursuit fix saturated
its win count (both top rungs swept the old 24-match slate; Expert
16% faster); its slate widened to 80 when the slide's re-roll showed
the 24-match sample inverting rungs the 80-match truth ordered; and
the fog-honest duel gate moved to a seat-swapped 20-seed combined
majority when its five-seed fixture proved to carry a degenerate
identical pair (the wide truth had IMPROVED under the tether, 26/40
vs 21/40, while the sample flipped).
