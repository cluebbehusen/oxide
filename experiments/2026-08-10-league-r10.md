# League r10 — consolidation: every pressure held, zero shaping

Experiment: end the r7/r8/r9 seesaw (each single-focus phase traded
away the previous phase's gain: r8 fixed the faction confound and
rush defense but not reach; r9 fixed fabricator reach and deepened
tiers but regressed rush defense to 45%) with one phase that holds
every constraint simultaneously, on the beta.4 Array rebalance.

- Command: `uv run league.py --name r10-consolidation
  --initialize-from runs/r9-intel-pressure/pool/ckpt-01800.pt
  --anchor runs/prior-v9.pt --collection episodes --updates 300
  --mix "self=0.25,past=0.10,overseer=0.25,rusher=0.20,ffa=0.05,team=0.15"
  --map-mix "fixed=.35,random=.30,grand=.35"
  --faction-mix "ff=.25,fc=.25,cf=.25,cc=.25" --probe-every 100`
  (workspace 0.15.0-beta.4; driver carries the Array rebalance:
  cost 120 -> 90, radar ring 16 -> 20).
- Deliberately unshaped: no --structure-bonus, no --mix-bonus
  (r5 established tier preference survives with zero shaping; the
  rebalanced tree must carry the climb on win signal alone, making
  r10 both consolidation and the end-state confirmation).
- Rusher jobs keep the expert execution pin; overseer stays at 0.25
  so intel pressure persists while the rush answer re-hardens.

Result: the consolidation held both pressures in training (paired
torch evals up to 1.0 overseer / 0.7 rusher) and teching stayed at
8-9/10 episodes with zero shaping — the rebalanced tree carries the
climb on win signal alone, confirming r5's finding at full scale.
Native Q12 cups across the phase (beta.4): ckpt-01975 is the peak —
a PERFECT 60/60 vs the Overseer (30F/30C) with rusher 57%
(campaign-strong), while final ckpt-02100 softened to 90%/47%. The
rebalanced Array was adopted organically (3.5 -> 5.4 builds/update
early, settling ~3.9) and ckpt-01975 passes every reach floor
including the re-anchored array minimum.

Fun gate on ckpt-01975: fails exactly one metric — per-seat value
entropy p25 at 1.33 bits vs the 1.35 floor (a bombard-leaning lower
quartile; dealt share 35%). Everything else is the campaign's best
game by a wide margin: 85-87 fight windows (r8: 47), finish latency
6,287 med (r8: 8,122), tier-2/3 value share 65/83/75, extractor
tenure 7%, and the deep roster (sappers, interceptors, bombers)
actually appears in play.

Follow-up (r11/r12): a short diversity polish from ckpt-01975 using
the production-head entropy regularizer — a training knob, not a
gate change. r11 (0.004, 60 updates) cleared the spam floor (p25
1.53) and delivered campaign-best balanced rush defense (65%,
21F/18C) but scattered support-structure builds (array reach 16%,
reclaimer 18.2%, both under the 25% floors) and cost Overseer edge
(85%). r12 titrated 0.002 for 40 updates: rush oscillated (52% at
update 25, 37% at 40) without a clean win — abandoned.

Diagnosis of the p25 failure itself (probe-01975.json, per-seat
decomposition): the low quartile is NOT bombard spam. It is (a)
STUNTED SEATS on team/FFA maps — contained early, never developing,
51-64% sentinel value and nothing else, match capped at 40k with no
result — plus (b) a few capped bombard-fortress standoffs (74-81%
bombard). The floor is catching a real pathology (degenerate
multi-seat sieges and failure-to-develop stalls), so it stands as
written. Two supporting checks: the arena acquits the Bombard
itself (naked stacks lose every cost-normalized duel — scuttlers,
lancers, sentinels, buzzards — so massed bombard play only works
properly screened, i.e. the meta is combined-arms, not a broken
unit), and r8 passes the same floor at 1.53 while playing a far
shallower game.
