# Domain regression guide

Use the relevant boundary cases below with the owning source and focused tests.
Current policy, constants and ownership live in
[Bot architecture](../../../../docs/bot-architecture.md); desired evolution
lives in [Bot strategy](../../../../docs/bot-strategy.md). This guide selects
evidence, not a second ruleset. Ordinary simulation contracts still apply to
every command.

## Shared allocation and production

Exercise exact live units, paid queue occurrences, producer schedules, capital,
builders and footprints across competing domains. Derive proposals and retained
obligations from the same resource snapshot. Test acceptance and subsequent
commitment without a rerank. Paid work survives release of unpaid demand.

Use `resources/production` tests for necessary capacity bounds, FIFO readiness,
deadlines, egress, restricted producer/kind pairs and paid occurrence
exclusions. An excluded paid item still delays its lane. Completion during the
deadline's production phase cannot count as present in that tick's earlier
observation. Test exact production feasibility through the shared funded
refinement service, including producer-subset overload beside unrelated spare
capacity and deferred work that must not trigger recovery. Keep independent
small exhaustive oracles when optimizing the search; do not restore an unbounded
runtime solver for tests.

Use allocation and Brain tests for retained forecast-to-current maturation,
protected spending, future-lane reservations, preemption and producer loss.
Preserve fixed Lift, reconnaissance and standing-force bookings when funding
changes. Connected procurement retains its deadline and actual investments but
may change unpaid factories and times. Test earlier purchases, replacement
factories, completed providers reducing demand, rollback retries, and retained
paid ownership across revisions. Deferred scheduling must not release unpriced
connected demand into discretionary spending. A genuinely unfundable retained
lift enters bounded recovery instead of extending its deadline. Test complete
combined footprint sets, including a four-site closure where all pairs and
triples retain a route. Verify same-decision cancellations in placement and
worker queries, and that Stop still reserves its worker against construction.

## Economy, core protection and expansion

Cover exact HP-weighted core boundaries, live/queued/same-think counting,
operation exclusions, every blocked spend channel and a later core loss. A think
that starts below the core remains gated even if same-think orders project it
above the floor. Existing operations may advance without buying; new admission
stays closed. Test current-threat emergency ground and ground-attack-air
evidence against memory, radar, public-prior and pure air-to-air negatives.

Exercise the exact authored home frame, living starting Foundry, full footprint
exploration and safe builder. Keep rally points off known frames. Cover shallow
Sentinel timing, exact remaining escrow, no reachable ground objective, and
preservation of paid queues and foundations after core loss.

For investment and Foundry changes, cover finite safe work, initial worker
travel, useful prerequisite demand, missing-chain costs, capacity bottlenecks,
refit downtime and source-specific lost income. Count paid/deferred supply once.
An already-covered current need cannot lend confidence to speculative demand.
Foundry saving and dispatch must share the same exact worker/site assessment;
test expiry, lost core, changed knowledge, lease theft and blocked recovery.
Buying missing security must reduce outstanding cost and cash together. A newly
shallow Sentinel cannot repeatedly revoke accepted construction. Automatic Bay
spending remains ordinary simulation behavior.

## Standing force and connected operations

Test disconnected fronts needing the same kind, exact service routes and
producer exits, lower/higher-tier substitutes, and inventory claimed by other
operations. Derive conditional Standing contexts for Connected absence, minimum
and marginal scales. Combine retained and new paid ownership by maximum
multiplicity. Losing a retained Connected schedule must remove its obligation
and dependent contexts together before rederiving unconditional Standing work.

Test future purchases beside affordable alternatives, maturity, lost income or
factory, vanished demand and emergency preemption. Siege preparation against
fortifications must not make every purchase urgent or suppress useful work for
another need. Optional connected scale must preserve stronger producer slots.

For connected packages, test canonical target fallback, complete cluster scope,
all current covering anti-air, exact reconnaissance footprint coverage, route
and egress agreement, funded FIFO schedules and fixed preparation deadlines.
More resources, usable production or preparation time must not revoke admission
or reduce capped useful capability for identical evidence. Every personality
retains both air and siege. Freeze exact assignments at commitment and
distinguish cohort minimum loss from loss of the exact required scout.

Preflight must reproduce authoritative orientation, center snapping, group
spreading, producer doorsteps and legal firing stands. Scheduled suppression
members without positions require both possible spread scans; exact Attack
members do not use group spread. Do not double-count mandatory mobile anti-air
suppression as optional collateral. Cover bounded abort and recovery through
both pure planner and composed controller tests.

## Reconnaissance, support and battlefield memory

Use full observation histories and hidden-state twins. Displacement requires two
real sightings; radar cannot reveal composition. Preparation, outbound work,
recall and cooldown maintenance must continue without discretionary attention.

Freeze the question, consumer, useful deadline and dispatch identity at
acceptance. One exact paid occurrence cannot answer two independent assignments.
A missing completed observer must not steal a later question's newborn. Public
bootstrap priors differ from reactions to new sightings. Late but living scouts
retain recall ownership; they are not deaths. Test loss recovery, cooldown plus
quiet interval, fresh route validation and unrelated concurrent questions
through Brain. Expired timers never purchase replacements by themselves. Credit
overlapping point questions only if one route answers both complete footprints
in time; contested sweeps and recent positive contacts do not receive that
credit.

Support tests compare finite repair demand after live and delayed reachable
service. Separate repair debits from replacement value. Keep exact active worker
ownership and stop unfunded voluntary repair before protected purchases,
including between macro-admission boundaries. Protective deployments request
missing screen/anti-air through Standing Force. Relief preparation must not
launch before shared acceptance, and active withdrawal must survive attention
limits. Preserve home strength and tactical minimums without personality caps.

For Executive ground missions, test atomic staging splits, both minimums,
partially reserved lift payloads and an available defensive remainder. Cover two
pressured fronts, an unreachable leader, unchanged pressure elsewhere and
recovery that does not reacquire a chase. Maintenance withdrawal wins over new
mission directives. Multiple remembered building sites share placeholder ids;
reacquisition must preserve exact site, ownership, footprint orientation,
deadline and outcome history across real-id replacement.

Journals distinguish completed service, progress, aborted preparation, losses,
invalidation and uncertainty. Own cargo proves presence but not availability.
Shared air/lift credit must not double-count delivery or claim handed-off
troops. Experience tests cover neutral, both band thresholds, saturation, decay,
staggered contributions, counterevidence and shared-credit replacement. Later
reports cannot renew old ages. Frozen sites link failed approaches through fog;
one route failure cannot establish broad doctrine.

Work retries use dispatched worker/target receipts. Distinguish death,
preemption, exhaustion, funding loss, occupied footprints and blocked routes.
Remembered buildings cannot prove current occupation. Fresh foundations must not
displace active builders; movement/completion release their work tiles, while
nonblocking charges do not reserve them.

## Defense, contested harvest, air and lift

Defense tests cover all legal roles through shared allocation, exact quoted
kind/site/builder reuse, actual coverage geometry, unfinished reservations,
diminishing return, builder/resource egress and ordinary prerequisites. Array
value is novel usable information, not weapon damage. Test exact builder
readiness, expired-on-arrival leaders and useful runners-up, bounded quotation,
small-map partial coverage, and off-map/Peak negatives.

Contested harvest requires a matching own or visible allied worker loss or HP
change to promote an incident into durable quarantine. A combat wreck alone is
insufficient. Keep overlapping incident centers distinct. Sweep every unseen
safe tile of the exact region; danger, unreachable targets and no progress
require recall and observed safe return before retry or release. General retry
expiry cannot clear quarantine, and an idle field scout is not returned home.

Lift tests cover payload/carrier scaling during Provision, disjoint manifests
frozen at Boarding, usable landing space, home defense, shared quorum and
bounded phase recovery. Remembered-target carrier savings require optimistic
honest routing, usable production and payload, and no existing carrier. They are
a capital floor, not permission to start the operation or claim riders.
Admission must account for the floor once and release it when a premise fails.

Island air tests retain the documented readiness gate without turning it into a
force cap. Screen/bomber scaling stops at commitment. Air/lift coordination uses
explicit target-specific holds and release/abort evidence, never missing hidden
state. Parked aircraft occupy the ground body domain for targeting while route
and procurement reasoning retain their flight domain. Ordinary Move holds the
wing on pads and the strike order takes it off. Test lost dispatched scouts in
Recon and Assemble separately from empty uncommitted scout slots.

## Difficulty and replay boundaries

Exercise shared admission boundaries separately from private think cadence and
reaction/commitment delay. Faster controllers cannot freeze different rosters
merely by sampling an unavailable tick. Check monotone schedules, memory,
attention, estimates and core floors independently of stance/personality.

New Match may choose seeds; Restart, Rematch and replay resume preserve the
recorded profile. Reconstruct memory from the authoritative recorded prefix.
Read-only playback replays commands, so it does not validate changed reasoning.
